Ada Dies 2026 - Bowling Activity


This year the celebration of the Ada Dies will again not go unnoticed. We have again arranged an amazing activity, and this year this will be on Saturday the 11th of April.

The activity will take place at Vier Hoog Eindhoven (Vijfkamplaan 6A Eindhoven) and will start at 14:00. We will host a bowling activity where you can enjoy some light-hearted bowling, catching up with fellow alumni and also possibly some drinks and snacks for the duration of 2 hours!

The activity will end at 16:30, after which we invite you for some additional drinks at the GEWIS bar on the TU/e campus. The costs of the activity will be €10 per attending member and you only have to subscribe for the bowling activity.

Please subscribe and pay before the 6th of April, such that we can ensure we can accommodate all members that want to join. We hope that you are all joining us at the Ada dies celebration this year!
