Aday 2026 - Hado & BBQ


We are very excited to announce the details of this year's Aday! This year, we will be playing Hado — an exciting and innovative form of VR dodgeball where you compete in teams using augmented reality headsets and motion sensors. A unique mix of gaming and physical activity, guaranteed to bring out your competitive spirit and create lots of laughs! More information about Hado can be found here.

The Aday will take place on Saturday the 11th of July, starting at 13:00. Afterwards, we will continue with a BBQ at the university, starting at 16:00 — a great opportunity to relax, catch up, and enjoy good food together. Partners are welcome to join as well!

We will also create a WhatsApp group for participants so you can coordinate travel plans or get in touch beforehand.

Looking for Alumni Contributions


The Department of Mathematics and Computer Science is currently updating its website and developing new communication materials, and we would love to hear from alumni of our master's programs.

We are looking for alumni who would be willing to share their journey after graduation, whether that involves further study, a career in industry, entrepreneurship, research, or another path. By sharing your experiences, you can help current and prospective students gain insight into the opportunities that our programs can lead to.

The materials we are looking for may range from written stories and testimonials to short self-recorded videos, depending on your interest and availability.

If you would like to contribute, please contact e.donnelly1@tue.nl and we would be happy to share more details.

Thank you for helping us showcase the experiences and achievements of our alumni community.
