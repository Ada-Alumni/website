TU/e Lustrum Alumni Day


On Saturday the 17th of October, the TU/e Lustrum Alumni Day takes place. Below is the specific program for Mathematics and Computer Science:

14:00 - 15:30 — Keynote by Prof. Joaquin Vanschoren
Prof. Joaquin Vanschoren, Associate Professor of Machine Learning at TU/e, will give a keynote on building AI systems that learn continually and democratize machine learning. He is the founder of OpenML and started the NeurIPS Datasets and Benchmarks track.

16:00 - 17:30 — Nexus Lab Tours
Discover the new Nexus Lab, the interdisciplinary innovation space of the Department of Mathematics and Computer Science. Explore hands-on setups in robotics, cybersecurity, AI, autonomous systems, and more during three guided rounds.

Campus Pub Crawl of Student Bars
After the program, join the campus pub crawl to visit the various student bars and reconnect with fellow alumni over a drink.

More information and registration can be found here:
https://alumnilustrumday.tue-events.nl/
